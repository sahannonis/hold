Put your thumb on the circle. Don't let go.

That's the only rule in Hold. The timer runs as long as your thumb stays down. Lift it, even for a split second, and the run is over.

It sounds easy. It isn't.

THE CIRCLE MOVES
After 30 seconds the circle starts to drift. Slide your thumb to stay inside it. Leave it for more than a second and you're out.

THE SCREEN LIES
The longer you last, the harder Hold tries to break you. A fly crawls toward your thumb. A voice whispers "let go." A prize appears that you only have to tap. The timer freezes. The screen goes black. The circle splits in two, and only one of them is real.

FEEL THE HEAT
The circle starts cool blue and burns hotter the longer you hold, through violet and magenta to orange.

EARN YOUR RANK
Every run earns a title: Twitchy, Nervous, Steady, Stubborn, Stone, Monk, Statue, Glacier, and Legend. Beat your best, then copy the dare and send it to a friend.

No accounts. No ads. No internet needed. Nothing you do leaves your phone.

How long can you last?

Privacy Policy

Hold by 12th House Protocol · Effective 18 October 2026

Hold does not collect, store or share any personal information. Nothing you do in the game leaves your phone.

What the app keeps

To show your progress, Hold saves a few things on your own device only:

Saved on your phoneWhy
Your best time and its rank titleShown on the home and result screens
Your last 8 run timesShown as the recent-runs list
Sound and vibration on or offRemembers your settings

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names or email addresses.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or advertising IDs.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • No internet connection is used while you play.

Permissions

Hold asks Android for permission to vibrate, which it uses for short buzzes during a run. You can turn vibration off on the home screen.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Hold, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes everything it saved. There is nothing stored anywhere else to delete.

Children

Hold is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

Future versions may add online leaderboards or ads. Before any update that sends data off your phone, we will update this page and the app's Google Play listing to say exactly what is collected and why.
